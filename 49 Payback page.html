Peak = average of the 3 highest billable hours on 3 different days (night 22–06 counts at {{pct(m.settings.nightFactor)}} %). "Without" is the house load as it would come from the grid without the system. Amounts in kr.

How it is calculated

  • Data: hourly grid import, house (AC) load and solar from grid-control-logs/energy-*.csv and the older daily logs. All loads are behind the inverter, so the AC load is what the house would import without the system.
  • Peak shaving: each month the capacity step from the real grid peaks vs. the step from the house load; saving = difference of the monthly step prices.
  • Solar: solar kWh × (energy price + grid energy charge of that hour).
  • Battery shift: (house load − grid import − solar) × price of that hour. Positive when import moves to cheaper night hours, negative for battery and inverter losses.
  • Energy price is one fixed value (no hourly spot prices in the data). The estimate of the time left is a straight line from the average saving per day: winter and summer months differ.
  • Not counted: backup power during grid outages, and price differences of hourly spot prices.
